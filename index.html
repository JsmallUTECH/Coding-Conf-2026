About Coding Conf

Coding Conf 2026 is a technology conference that brings together students, developers, programmers, and technology enthusiasts who share a passion for coding and innovation.

The conference provides an opportunity for attendees to explore new technologies, improve their programming knowledge, and connect with others in the tech community.

What to Expect

Attendees will have the opportunity to participate in discussions, learn about web development and software engineering, and gain valuable insights into the technology industry.

Who Should Attend?

Whether you are a beginner learning your first programming language, a university student developing your skills, or an experienced developer, Coding Conf 2026 welcomes everyone interested in technology.
